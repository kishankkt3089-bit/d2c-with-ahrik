Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does “24/7 operations” actually mean?
Our operations team monitors your seller accounts around the clock. Customer queries are responded to within 2 hours, account health alerts are addressed immediately, and pricing changes are tracked in real-time. You get a dedicated ops manager plus a team that covers all time zones and marketplace SLA windows.
How do you handle customer complaints and negative reviews?
We have a structured escalation framework: Level 1 (standard queries) resolved within 2 hours, Level 2 (refunds/replacements) within 6 hours, Level 3 (A-to-Z claims) within 24 hours with documented resolution plans. For negative reviews, we respond professionally, resolve root causes, and follow up to request updated feedback.
Do you handle FBA/FBF inventory issues?
Yes. We manage complete FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) and FBF (Fulfillment by Flipkart) inventory reconciliation, including damaged/lost inventory claims, stranded inventory resolution, and removal order management. Our team ensures you recover every rupee from warehouse discrepancies.
Can you prevent account suspensions?
Prevention is our primary focus. We proactively monitor all account health metrics, policy compliance requirements, and performance thresholds. When we detect risk signals, we take corrective action before they trigger suspensions. In the rare event of a suspension, we handle the complete reinstatement process.
