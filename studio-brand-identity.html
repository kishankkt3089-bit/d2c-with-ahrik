How It Works

Brand Identity Built in 4 Creative Phases

1

Brand Discovery

We deep-dive into your brand vision, target audience, category context, and competitor visual landscape to define your design direction and positioning.

2

Concept Development

Our designers present 3 distinct visual directions — each with logo, palette, and typography — so you choose the direction that resonates most with your brand DNA.

3

Identity Refinement

We refine the chosen direction through 3 rounds of structured feedback, expanding the system to include all logo variations, packaging, and asset library.

4

System Delivery

Final delivery includes all source files (Figma, AI, SVG, PNG), the complete Brand Style Guide PDF, and a usage training session for your team.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does brand identity design take?
Our standard brand identity process takes 3-4 weeks from kick-off to final delivery. This includes discovery, 3 concept directions, 3 feedback rounds, and complete system documentation. Expedited timelines (2 weeks) are available for brands with urgent launch requirements.
Do you design packaging as well?
Yes. All brand identity packages include up to 3 SKUs of packaging design — labels, boxes, or pouches depending on your product category. Packaging is designed to work across both digital (Amazon listing images) and physical (retail shelf) environments simultaneously.
What file formats will I receive?
You receive a complete source file package: Figma project files (design system), AI / EPS vector files (print-ready), SVG + PNG exports in multiple sizes (web/digital), and PDF exports (presentations). All files are organized into a structured folder system with naming conventions your team can follow without design expertise.
Can you refresh an existing brand identity?
Absolutely. Our Brand Audit & Refresh service starts with a comprehensive evaluation of your existing brand assets, identifies visual inconsistencies and positioning gaps, then evolves your identity with minimal disruption to existing brand recognition. This is ideal for brands at INR 1-5 Cr ARR looking to premiumize before their next growth phase.
