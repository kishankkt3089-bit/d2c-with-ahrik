Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What stage do you work with?
We typically work with D2C brands between INR 50L and INR 5 Cr ARR looking to raise pre-seed to Series A rounds. Brands with strong growth trajectories but lower revenue may qualify with exceptional unit economics. We do a readiness assessment before engagement to ensure our network is the right fit.
What types of investors are in your network?
Our network includes D2C-focused angel investors (typically INR 25L-2 Cr ticket sizes), early-stage VCs with consumer brand mandates (INR 2-10 Cr), family offices with retail investment interests, and strategic investors who are brand builders or distribution partners. We match you with investors based on category, stage, and check size alignment.
Do you guarantee meetings or funding?
We guarantee warm introductions to matched investors. Meetings and funding outcomes depend on your metrics, pitch quality, and market timing — which is why we spend significant time on pitch preparation and readiness assessment. Our goal is to put you in front of the right investors with the best possible context.
What is included in the due diligence data room?
We set up a secure, structured data room containing: financial statements (3 years), MIS reports, cap table, legal documents (MoA, shareholder agreements, trademark certificates), operational metrics dashboard, customer acquisition data, and a management team profile — all organized to the standard format expected by institutional investors.
