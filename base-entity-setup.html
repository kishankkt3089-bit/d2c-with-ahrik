Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I just stay a sole proprietor?
Sole proprietorship offers zero liability shield — if your brand is sued, your personal assets (savings, property) are at risk. Additionally, most investors, Amazon, Flipkart, and B2B clients prefer dealing with Private Limited companies for credibility and legal enforceability. Upgrading later is far more complex than getting it right from Day 1.
How long does the entire entity setup take?
With our streamlined process, GST registration typically completes within 7 working days. Private Limited incorporation takes 10-14 working days from document submission. Startup India and MSME registrations are processed in parallel — so you get everything simultaneously, not sequentially.
Do I need a CA separately after this?
No. Our team includes chartered accountants and legal advisors who handle everything in-house. You deal with one team, one point of contact, zero confusion. We also provide ongoing compliance advisory so you never get surprised by regulatory deadlines.
What if I want to change my entity structure later?
Converting a sole proprietorship or LLP to Private Limited later requires complex restructuring — new bank accounts, transfer of contracts, compliance history re-documentation, and often tax implications. Getting the structure right in Week 1 eliminates this future complexity entirely.
