Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What ROAS should I expect?
ROAS targets vary by category and AOV. Beauty/wellness brands typically achieve 3-5x ROAS at scale. Food/FMCG brands optimize for contribution margin rather than ROAS. We set ROAS targets based on your unit economics — ensuring campaigns are profitable at your specific COGS and margin structure, not generic benchmarks.
How much ad budget should I start with?
We recommend a minimum INR 1-2L/month for meaningful testing and learning. Under INR 1L, the learning phase is too slow to generate statistically significant insights. The ideal testing budget for a new campaign launch is INR 2-5L for the first month — this funds sufficient creative tests to identify 2-3 winning combinations before scaling.
Do you manage influencer marketing as well?
Yes. Our Influencer & UGC Marketing service runs alongside performance campaigns — building social proof assets that also feed back into your paid ad creative library. Nano and micro influencers (10K-200K followers) in your product category typically deliver the best engagement-to-cost ratio.
How do you handle iOS 14+ attribution issues?
We use server-side event tracking (Conversions API) alongside pixel tracking to maximize attribution accuracy post-iOS 14. We also use Google Analytics 4 for cross-platform attribution, UTM-based last-click analysis, and MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio) as a platform-agnostic ROAS metric for strategic budget decisions.
