Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Shopify vs WooCommerce — which should I use?
We recommend Shopify for brands under INR 5 Cr ARR wanting fast launch and low maintenance overhead. WooCommerce for brands needing deep customization at a lower platform cost. Custom headless stacks for brands at INR 10 Cr+ ARR needing maximum performance and unique experiences. We choose based on your specific goals, not platform preferences.
How long does a D2C storefront build take?
A standard D2C storefront takes 3-5 weeks from kick-off to launch — design week 1-2, development week 2-4, testing and launch week 5. Complex custom builds or marketplace integrations may take 6-8 weeks. We always prioritize a CRO-ready MVP launch over a delayed perfect launch.
Do you provide ongoing support after launch?
Yes. All storefront builds include 30 days of post-launch support for bug fixes, performance monitoring, and initial conversion optimization. Ongoing monthly retainer plans are available for brands wanting continuous CRO testing, feature additions, and technical maintenance.
Can you migrate my existing Shopify store?
Yes. We handle complete Shopify-to-Shopify migrations, WooCommerce-to-Shopify migrations, and custom platform migrations. This includes theme redesign, product data migration, SEO redirect mapping, and zero-downtime deployment — ensuring you lose no organic rankings or customer data during the transition.
