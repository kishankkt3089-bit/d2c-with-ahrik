Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is A+ Content and why does it matter?
A+ Content (Amazon) and Enhanced Brand Content (Flipkart) allows you to add rich media — comparison charts, lifestyle images, brand stories — to your product detail pages. Brands with A+ Content see 5-15% higher conversion rates because shoppers trust listings with premium visual content more than text-only listings.
How many keywords do you target per product?
We map 300-500+ relevant keywords per product through competitor analysis, search term reports, and marketplace auto-suggest mining. The top 20-30 high-volume keywords are strategically placed in titles and bullets, while the remaining long-tail keywords are optimized through backend search terms and A+ Content.
Can you optimize my existing listings or only new ones?
Both. We handle complete catalog rewrites for existing products and build optimized listings from scratch for new launches. For existing catalogs, we start with a full audit to identify the highest-impact optimization opportunities first.
How often do listings need to be updated?
We recommend quarterly keyword refreshes and seasonal content updates. Search trends change, competitors update their listings, and marketplace algorithms evolve. Our ongoing management includes monthly performance reviews and proactive optimization to maintain and improve your ranking positions.
