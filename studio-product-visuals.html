How It Works

Product Visuals Delivered in 4 Phases

1

Product Brief & Shot List

We define the shot list, scene compositions, background styles, and platform requirements (Amazon A+ requirements, Flipkart specs, Instagram aspect ratios) before any shooting begins.

2

AI Production or Physical Shoot

Based on your product category and timeline, we execute either an AI-powered photoshoot (48-hour delivery) or a physical lifestyle shoot (5-7 day delivery), or a hybrid combining both for complete coverage.

3

Post-Production & Editing

Every image goes through professional post-production — background removal, color grading, retouching, infographic overlay design — to meet the exacting standards of premium marketplace listings.

4

Delivery & Optimization

You receive all images in the exact formats and dimensions required by each platform, along with SEO-optimized alt-text and image sequence recommendations for maximum listing conversion.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between AI photoshoots and traditional photography?
Our standard brand identity process takes 3-4 weeks from kick-off to final delivery. This includes discovery, 3 concept directions, 3 feedback rounds, and complete system documentation. Expedited timelines (2 weeks) are available for brands with urgent launch requirements.
How many images do I get per SKU?
Standard deliveries include 8-12 images per SKU: 1 white background hero, 2-3 lifestyle shots, 2-3 infographic/detail shots, and 1-2 lifestyle or feature images. For A+ Content, we deliver an extended set of 12-15 images per SKU optimized for module-by-module presentation.
Can you do AI photoshoots for food & beverage products?
Yes. Food photography via AI is one of our specialties — generating appetizing, premium-quality food visuals with precise lighting, steam effects, and plating compositions that would cost 5-10x more in a traditional food photography studio. Results are indistinguishable from high-end physical shoots.
Do you shoot videos during product shoots?
Yes. Every physical product shoot includes optional 30-60 second video clips — suitable for Instagram Reels, Amazon product videos, and Meta ad creatives. These clips can be further edited into motion ads or brand content by our Visual Storytelling team.
