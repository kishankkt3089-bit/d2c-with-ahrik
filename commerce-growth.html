Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What ROAS can I expect from marketplace advertising?
Average ROAS varies by category, but our managed campaigns typically deliver 4-8x ROAS within 60-90 days. We optimize for profitable growth, not vanity ad spend. Initial ACoS may be higher during launch phases while we build organic velocity, but stabilizes as organic rankings improve.
How do you handle seasonal campaigns like Big Billion Days?
We plan seasonal campaigns 4-6 weeks in advance — including inventory forecasting, deal submissions, ad budget escalation plans, and promotional strategy. During sale events, our team runs real-time bid management and budget reallocation to maximize revenue capture during peak traffic windows.
What’s the difference between ACoS and TACoS?
ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) measures ad spend vs. ad-attributed revenue. TACoS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales) measures ad spend vs. total revenue including organic sales. We optimize for TACoS because it reflects the true efficiency of your advertising investment — great ads should lift organic sales too.
Do you manage Flipkart advertising too?
Yes. We manage the complete advertising stack across Amazon (Sponsored Products, Brands, Display, DSP) and Flipkart (Product Listing Ads, Smart ROI campaigns, Brand Ads). Strategy is unified across platforms but execution is optimized for each marketplace’s unique algorithm and audience behavior.
