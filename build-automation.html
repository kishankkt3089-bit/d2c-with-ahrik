Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Shopify vs WooCommerce — which should I use?
We recommend Shopify for brands under INR 5 Cr ARR wanting fast launch and low maintenance overhead. WooCommerce for brands needing deep customization at a lower platform cost. Custom headless stacks for brands at INR 10 Cr+ ARR needing maximum performance and unique experiences. We choose based on your specific goals, not platform preferences.
How long does a D2C storefront build take?
Standard automation implementations take 2-3 weeks from kick-off to full go-live. Simple chatbot implementations can be live in 7 days. Complex multi-system integrations (CRM + chatbot + analytics + inventory) take 4-5 weeks for full deployment and testing.
Do you provide ongoing support after launch?
Yes. All automation systems include a 30-day post-launch monitoring period with performance reviews. Monthly retainer plans include ongoing flow optimization, trigger refinement, and new automation additions as your operational needs evolve.
Can you migrate my existing Shopify store?
Yes. We connect your automation stack to any existing tools — Shopify, WooCommerce, Razorpay, Shiprocket, Zoho CRM, Klaviyo, WebEngage, and 50+ common D2C tech platforms. Our integration approach prioritizes API-native connections over fragile workarounds for maximum reliability.
