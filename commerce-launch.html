Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which marketplaces do you support for onboarding?
We handle onboarding across Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho, JioMart, Myntra, Ajio, and regional platforms like Snapdeal and FirstCry. We recommend the ideal marketplace mix based on your product category, target audience, and growth ambitions.
How long does the complete onboarding process take?
A standard multi-platform onboarding takes 5-7 business days from kick-off to first listing live. This includes registration, KYC verification, brand registry enrollment, store configuration, and initial catalog upload. Complex cases with trademark filing may take 2-3 weeks.
Do I need a trademark for brand registry?
For Amazon Brand Registry, a registered trademark is required. For Flipkart, a trademark application (TM status) is sufficient. If you don’t have either, our AHrik Base team can handle trademark filing in parallel while we proceed with basic seller onboarding.
What if my seller account was previously suspended?
We handle account reinstatement and appeal processes for suspended Amazon and Flipkart accounts. This includes root cause analysis, Plan of Action (POA) drafting, compliance documentation, and direct escalation support to get your selling privileges restored.
