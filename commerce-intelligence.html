Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of dashboards do you build?
We build custom dashboards that consolidate data from Amazon Seller Central, Flipkart Seller Hub, advertising consoles, and third-party tools into a single view. Key dashboards include: Revenue & Orders Overview, SKU-Level P&L, Advertising Performance, Search Rank Tracker, Competitor Comparison, and Inventory Health.
How do you track SKU-level profitability?
We calculate true profitability for every SKU by factoring in: product cost, marketplace commission, shipping fees, advertising spend (attributed), return rate cost, FBA fees, and promotional discounts. This gives you exact unit economics so you know which products to scale, which to optimize, and which to sunset.
What happens in monthly strategy sessions?
Monthly sessions are 60-90 minute deep dives covering: last month’s performance vs. targets, top/bottom performing SKUs, competitor movements, advertising efficiency trends, and action items for the next month. We come prepared with data, insights, and recommendations — you come with questions and priorities.
Can you benchmark us against competitors?
Yes. We track competitor pricing, bestseller ranks, review counts, advertising strategies, and new product launches across your key competitive set. Monthly competitor reports highlight where you’re winning, where you’re losing, and specific actions to gain market share in each category.
